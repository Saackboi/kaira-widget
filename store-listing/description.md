A lightweight, self-contained accessibility toolbar that works on every website. No setup, no configuration — just install and a floating button appears on every page you visit.

**What you can do:**

- **Vision:** high contrast, monochrome, dark contrast, saturation control, large cursor
- **Reading:** text sizing (4 levels), letter/word spacing, reading guide, dyslexia-friendly font
- **Navigation:** pause animations, highlight links, highlight headings, super focus mode, image alt text tooltips
- **Profiles:** one-click presets for seizures, low vision, ADHD, and dyslexia
- **Multi-language:** English and Spanish, auto-detected from your browser
- **Persistence:** all your preferences are saved and restored automatically

**How it works:**

Click the floating KAIRA tab on the right side of any page. A panel opens with all accessibility controls organized by category. Toggle what you need — changes apply instantly. Close the panel and your settings stay.

**Privacy:**

KAIRA does not collect, transmit, or store any of your data. All preferences live in your browser's local storage and never leave your device.

**Open source:**

The full source code is available at github.com/Saackboi/kaira-widget under the MIT license.

# Privacy Policy for KAIRA Accessibility Widget

**Last updated: July 2026**

## What data do we collect?

**Nothing.** KAIRA does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information or usage data.

## How does it work?

All preferences (text size, contrast settings, language, etc.) are saved to your browser's built-in `localStorage`. This data never leaves your device and is only used to restore your settings when you visit a page.

## Do we use external services?

The extension loads the Cinzel brand font from Google Fonts. No user data is sent to any external service. The dyslexia-friendly OpenDyslexic font is bundled with the extension when installed.

## Changes

If this policy changes in the future, we will update this document and note the change in the extension's version history.

## Contact

For questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/Saackboi/kaira-widget
